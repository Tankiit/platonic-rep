## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, methodology seat only): The study design, how data are collected and handled, how statistical results are reported (including variability and significance), and whether another group could reproduce the work must all reach the normal peer-review standard of the field. From an editorial-oversight position I note that this dimension belongs entirely to the methodology seat. I will not score it, but I will check that the panel's overall view stays consistent with it.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, domain seat only): Every claim must agree with current evidence in the field. Prior work must be described fairly and correctly, and domain terms and reported results must contain no factual errors. Only the domain seat scores this. My editorial interest is limited to how domain errors, if found, would affect the contribution's standing.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, devil's-advocate and methodology seats): The central thesis must not contradict itself, the evidence must actually support what is claimed, and no reasoning fallacy may undermine the main argument. The devil's advocate owns this and the methodology seat may also score it. I do not score it here.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, perspective seat only): Readers from neighbouring fields should be able to follow the framing, definitions, and implications, and any claim that crosses disciplines needs real support. The perspective seat alone scores this. Because it is high priority, a block here pushes the panel toward major revision.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by and eligible only to the Journal-Fit seat): This covers how the manuscript is organised, how clearly it explains its ideas, the quality of its figures and tables, and whether it follows the formatting and length rules of the target venue. As the owner, I judge whether the presentation lets a venue reader follow and assess the work. Because the dimension is non-mandatory, the worst outcome is a block that forces revision, never a fatal rejection.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by and eligible only to the Journal-Fit seat): The manuscript must sit within the configured venue's scope and make a contribution that is original and significant for that venue's readers. As owner, I judge scope, novelty against existing work, significance, and whether the claimed contribution is actually delivered. Because this dimension is mandatory, a block forces major revision and a fatal block forces rejection.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A logical flow from title through abstract, introduction, body, and conclusion; notation and terms defined before first use; figures and tables that are legible, labelled, and referenced in the text; compliance with venue page, format, and anonymisation conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition so unclear that a competent venue reader cannot reconstruct the main claim, method, or results, or a breach of venue formatting, length, or anonymisation rules that would block consideration.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity problems such as undefined notation, uncaptioned or hard-to-read figures, redundant or misordered sections, or minor departures from venue conventions that need revision but leave the work assessable.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: A clearly stated core contribution placed against the closest prior work; a topic within the configured venue's scope; a credible case for significance to that readership; agreement between the contribution claimed in the framing and what the body actually delivers.
what_triggers_block: The claimed contribution is substantially overstated relative to what is delivered, or novelty over closely related prior work is not established, but a narrower, honestly scoped contribution could be salvaged through revision.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are present, but significance is argued thinly, positioning against neighbouring work is incomplete, or the framing promises somewhat more than the results support.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript falls outside the configured venue's scope, or its central contribution is already established in prior work or absent altogether, so that no revision within the paper's present scope could yield an original contribution for this readership.
