## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, methodology-owned): From an adversarial stance, this dimension asks whether the design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive a hostile methods referee at the field's bar. I will not score it, but any design choice that silently manufactures the headline result is a challenge I may still raise as a finding.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, domain-owned): This dimension asks whether claims match the current evidence base, whether prior work is represented fairly rather than as a convenient strawman, and whether domain terminology and cited results are stated correctly. I will not score it, but misrepresentation of a rival position that the core argument leans on remains reportable.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, DA-owned and methodology-eligible): This is my scoring remit. It asks whether the core thesis holds together internally, whether each inferential step from evidence to conclusion is valid, and whether the central argument is free of fallacies, hidden premises, scope inflation, or a more parsimonious rival explanation that the evidence fits at least as well. A negative or non-identifiability thesis is held to the same standard: the argument must show what it claims cannot be identified, not merely that one route to identification failed.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, perspective-owned): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions, and implications are legible to adjacent-field readers and whether interdisciplinary claims are backed rather than asserted. I will not score it; I may only flag a definitional slippage across fields if it breaks the core argument.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, EIC-owned): This dimension asks whether organisation, exposition, figures, tables, and venue conventions are adequate. I will not score it, and I will not inflate presentation issues into argumentative defects.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, EIC-owned): This dimension asks whether the manuscript fits the configured venue and makes an original, significant contribution for its readership. I will not score it; a "so what" challenge belongs in my findings only insofar as it exposes that the claimed contribution does not follow from the argument.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated core thesis follows step by step from the presented definitions, theory, and experiments; whether formal results and empirical evidence actually support the scope of the headline claim; hidden premises, equivocation between formal and informal notions of key terms, internal contradictions between sections, overgeneralization from specific settings, and unaddressed rival explanations that fit the same evidence.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing inferential gap, contradiction between sections, or unaddressed more-parsimonious rival explanation that leaves the central claim unsupported as stated but is repairable by narrowing scope, adding a missing argument step, or supplying evidence that discriminates against the rival.
what_triggers_warn: Localized logical leaps, scope overreach in secondary claims, or ambiguous use of central terms that weaken but do not sever the link between evidence and the central thesis.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper's own formal results or data contradict the core thesis, or the central conclusion rests on a premise shown false or circular within the manuscript, such that no revision short of abandoning the main claim restores coherence.
