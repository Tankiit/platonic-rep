## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility support must clear the normal peer-review bar of the field. From a cross-disciplinary standpoint, this matters because readers from adjacent fields can only reuse or replicate a result if the procedure is fully specified. I am not eligible to score this dimension and will leave it to the methodology reviewer.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat): claims must match current evidence in the field, prior work must be represented faithfully, and domain terms and results must be stated without factual error. For outside readers, accurate terminology is the precondition for any interdisciplinary transfer, but judging it belongs to the domain reviewer, not to me.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate with methodology also eligible): the central thesis must be internally consistent, supported by its evidence, and free of fallacies that would undermine it. A coherent argument is what lets a reader from another discipline follow the reasoning without insider knowledge, but logic and consistency checks fall outside my remit.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by me as the perspective seat): the framing, definitions, and stated implications must be accessible to readers in neighbouring fields, and any claim that crosses disciplinary boundaries must be backed by evidence or argument rather than asserted. This is the one dimension I score. I will judge whether key concepts are defined so a non-specialist can follow them, whether links to adjacent communities are justified, and whether the broader implications are argued rather than gestured at.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief): the manuscript's organisation, clarity, figure and table quality, and adherence to venue conventions. Clear exposition supports adjacent-field accessibility, but the editor scores this dimension, not me.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief): the manuscript must suit the configured venue and make an original, significant contribution to that venue's readership. Significance to neighbouring communities can inform this judgement, but the score belongs to the editor.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Core technical terms defined operationally for non-specialists; explicit, justified mapping between concepts borrowed from different communities; stated scope conditions for where conclusions transfer; implications for adjacent-field practitioners argued with evidence; acknowledgement of neighbouring literatures that study the same phenomenon under different names.
what_triggers_block: A central interdisciplinary claim or concept bridge on which the paper's stated implications depend is asserted without any supporting evidence or argument, or relies on an undefined term that adjacent-field readers cannot reconstruct, so that the cross-field conclusion cannot be evaluated by its intended outside audience.
what_triggers_warn: Cross-field framing is broadly supported but some definitions are left implicit, some scope or transfer conditions are unstated, or claimed implications for neighbouring fields are plausible yet only partially argued, leaving gaps an adjacent-field reader would need to fill unaided.
