## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): From an editorial-oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility provisions reach the bar a peer-reviewed venue in this field expects. It is outside my scoring remit; I will rely on the methodology seat's judgement and only note where methodological gaps bear on overall contribution.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat): This dimension asks whether the manuscript's claims are consistent with current evidence in the domain, whether prior work is represented fairly and correctly, and whether domain terminology and reported results are free of factual error. I do not score it; the domain seat owns it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, scored by the devil's-advocate and methodology seats): This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence presented actually supports the claims made, and whether any logical fallacy undermines the core argument. I do not score it, though over-promising relative to delivered evidence also informs my contribution judgement under D6.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by the perspective seat): This dimension asks whether the framing, definitions, and implications are accessible to readers from adjacent fields, and whether any interdisciplinary claims are adequately substantiated. I do not score it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by me): This dimension asks whether the manuscript is well organised, clearly written, supported by legible and informative figures and tables, and compliant with the venue's formatting and presentation conventions. It is mine to score, and because it is non-mandatory a block here can drive at most a revision outcome, never rejection.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by me): This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs at the configured venue and offers an original and significant contribution suited to that venue's readership. It is mine to score; because it is mandatory, a fatal block here would on its own drive a reject decision under the contract's failure conditions, so the fatal threshold must be reserved for defects no revision could repair.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Logical section ordering that moves from question to method to evidence to conclusion; consistent notation and terminology; figures and tables that are readable, labelled, and referenced where used; title, abstract, and conclusion that match one another; compliance with venue page limits, anonymisation, and template conventions.
what_triggers_block: The exposition is disorganised or unclear enough that a competent reader cannot reconstruct the main claims or the experimental setup, or the submission violates a hard venue formatting rule such as page limit, template, or anonymisation.
what_triggers_warn: Exposition is followable but has localised problems such as undefined notation, inconsistent terminology, unreadable or unreferenced figures, an abstract that overstates the conclusion, or prose padding that obscures key results.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the research question sits within the venue's statistical machine learning scope; a clearly stated contribution distinguishable from prior work; evidence the contribution is significant for the venue's readership; alignment between promised and delivered contribution.
what_triggers_block: The claimed novelty is substantially overlapped by existing work without clear differentiation, or the delivered contribution falls materially short of what the title and abstract promise, but a revision reframing or adding evidence could plausibly fix it.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is real and in scope but its significance is understated or overstated, positioning against closely related work is thin, or the readership payoff is asserted rather than demonstrated.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is out of scope for the venue entirely, or its central contribution is already established in prior literature such that no revision could yield an original contribution.
