## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): Seen adversarially, this dimension asks whether the experimental design, data handling, statistical reporting and reproducibility affordances would hold up if a skeptical referee tried to reproduce the reported numbers. A confound, a leak between conditions, or reporting that hides variance would make every downstream conclusion suspect. The methodology seat scores it. I will still report any methodological weakness I find that bears on the logic of the argument.

D2 (domain_accuracy): Seen adversarially, this dimension asks whether the paper's statements about prior work and established results in its field are correct, or whether a misstatement makes a contribution look novel or a result look supported when it is not. The domain seat scores it. I may still raise a misrepresentation that props up the central thesis.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This is the dimension I own and score. It asks whether the core thesis holds together: each inferential step must follow from the evidence presented, there must be no hidden premise, circular reasoning or internal contradiction, and the conclusions must not outrun the data. My job is to build the strongest counter-narrative. I will look for a more parsimonious explanation of the reported findings, check whether correlation is being presented as mechanism, and check whether results from a limited setting are stated as general laws.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): Seen adversarially, this dimension asks whether the definitions and implications the paper offers to adjacent fields are actually substantiated, or are asserted bridges that would fail under scrutiny from those fields. The perspective seat scores it. I will only flag interdisciplinary claims whose failure would break the main argument.

D5 (writing_and_structure): This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, figure and table quality, and venue conventions. The EIC scores it. I will raise it only if unclear exposition hides a logical gap or makes a claim impossible to evaluate.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension asks whether the work fits the configured venue and whether the contribution is original and significant enough for that readership. The EIC scores it. My "so what?" stress test feeds it as challenge material only, not as a score.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether each step from the stated premises and the reported evidence to the headline conclusions is valid. This means looking for hidden assumptions, internal contradictions between sections, circular use of the same quantity as both evidence and criterion, correlational evidence presented as a causal or mechanistic explanation, conclusions stated more broadly than the tested settings support, and rival explanations the paper never addresses that would explain the same results more simply.
what_triggers_block: A headline conclusion does not follow from the evidence presented, or the paper's own reported results contradict a stated claim, but the core thesis could be saved by reframing, adding the missing analysis, or narrowing the claim.
what_triggers_warn: Secondary claims overreach the evidence, an unstated assumption carries real weight without justification, or a plausible alternative explanation goes unaddressed, while the main inferential chain stays intact.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis rests on a premise that is demonstrably false or circular, or a simpler alternative explanation fits the presented data better than the proposed account, so the core contribution cannot be saved by revision.
