## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory): From an adversarial standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances could survive a hostile referee at the field's normal bar. The challenge is whether the design choices quietly favour the hoped-for result, whether reported effects come with uncertainty that makes them interpretable, and whether a third party could rerun the work. It is owned by the methodology seat; I do not score it, but evidence-backed weaknesses I find here will still be reported as findings.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory): This dimension tests whether the claims match current domain evidence, whether prior work is represented fairly rather than strawmanned or selectively cited, and whether domain terminology and reported results are free of factual error. Adversarially, the question is whether the paper's positioning depends on misreading or omitting contrary literature. The domain seat scores it; I will not plan a score for it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory): This is my scored dimension. It asks whether the core thesis holds together internally, whether each step from evidence to conclusion is valid, and whether any fallacy, hidden assumption, circularity, or logical leap undermines the central argument. Adversarially, I will try to build the strongest counter-narrative, test whether the stated conclusions exceed what the evidence licenses, look for internal contradictions between sections, and check whether correlational or descriptive evidence is presented as mechanistic or causal.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions, and implications are accessible to readers from neighbouring fields and whether any interdisciplinary claims are actually substantiated rather than asserted. The adversarial angle is whether cross-field claims rest on borrowed vocabulary without the supporting evidence. It belongs to the perspective seat and I will not score it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal): This dimension concerns organisation, clarity of exposition, figure and table quality, and adherence to venue conventions. Adversarially, the relevant question is whether presentation choices obscure weaknesses or make claims hard to verify. The EIC seat scores it; I will not.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory): This dimension asks whether the manuscript fits the configured venue and makes an original, significant contribution for that readership. The adversarial version is the "so what?" test: whether the contribution is incremental, already known, or trivially implied by prior results. The EIC seat scores it; I do not, though I may report contribution-level counter-arguments as findings.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether every headline conclusion follows from the presented evidence through valid steps, with hidden assumptions stated, rival explanations addressed, scope of claims matched to scope of evidence, and no section contradicting another.
what_triggers_block: A central claim is not supported by the presented evidence or depends on an unstated assumption or unaddressed rival explanation, but the gap could be repaired by narrowing the claim, adding the missing argument, or running a targeted control.
what_triggers_warn: Secondary claims overreach the evidence, some hidden assumptions or alternative explanations go unacknowledged, or minor internal inconsistencies exist, while the core thesis remains supported.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper's own reported evidence contradicts its core thesis, or the central argument is circular or rests on a demonstrably false premise, such that no revision short of a different thesis could rescue it.
