## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor) asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility support meet the peer-review standard of its field. Seen from outside the field, that standard decides whether readers in neighbouring areas can trust and reuse the empirical findings without having to re-derive the setup. This dimension is mandatory and belongs to the methodology seat. I will not score it.

D2 (domain_accuracy) asks whether the claims agree with current domain evidence, whether prior work is represented faithfully, and whether domain terminology and results are stated without factual error. Accurate terminology is also what lets an adjacent-field reader map the paper's concepts onto their own vocabulary. This dimension is mandatory and belongs to the domain seat. I will not score it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence) asks whether the central thesis is internally consistent, whether the evidence actually supports the claims, and whether any fallacy undermines the main argument. A coherent argument is the minimum needed for any cross-field takeaway to carry over. This dimension is mandatory and belongs to the devil's-advocate seat, with the methodology seat also eligible. I will not score it.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance) asks whether a reader from an adjacent field can follow the framing, definitions, and implications, and whether every claim that reaches beyond the paper's home discipline is backed by evidence. Readers I have in mind include statisticians, cognitive scientists, and practitioners who deploy uncertainty estimates. This is a high-priority dimension and it is my own. I will judge it on three things: whether key constructs are defined without relying on in-group shorthand, whether implications for neighbouring communities are stated and bounded, and whether any analogy or transfer claim across fields rests on evidence rather than assertion.

D5 (writing_and_structure) asks whether the manuscript is well organised, clearly explained, has good figures and tables, and follows venue conventions. Clear structure lowers the cost of entry for outside readers, but this normal-priority dimension belongs to the editor-in-chief seat. I will not score it.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution) asks whether the manuscript suits the configured venue and makes an original, significant contribution for that venue's readers. The breadth of the contribution beyond its home niche matters to this question. The dimension is mandatory and belongs to the editor-in-chief seat. I will not score it.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Core constructs (alignment, agreement, uncertainty, representation similarity) are defined so that an adjacent-field reader can follow them; implications for neighbouring communities are stated explicitly and bounded; any interdisciplinary or transfer claim cites evidence or is clearly marked as speculative; assumptions that may not hold outside the home discipline are disclosed.
what_triggers_block: A headline interdisciplinary or cross-field implication is asserted as established without any supporting evidence, or a core construct is left undefined so that adjacent-field readers cannot tell what the main claim means.
what_triggers_warn: Framing depends on in-group jargon or implicit conventions that are never explained, or implications for adjacent fields or practitioners are overstated, unbounded, or missing even though they are clearly relevant.
