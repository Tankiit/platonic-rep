## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by methodology): From a domain-accuracy vantage, this dimension asks whether the experimental design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility provisions reach the standard a statistical machine learning venue expects of empirical uncertainty-quantification work. It is not mine to score; I note only that domain claims I evaluate rest on whatever empirical procedure this dimension certifies.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by domain): This is my dimension. It asks whether the paper's claims are consistent with the current evidence base in uncertainty quantification and representation similarity, whether prior work (on representational alignment and convergence, similarity indices, epistemic versus aleatoric uncertainty, and disagreement- or ensemble-based uncertainty) is represented faithfully and attributed to its original sources, and whether field terminology and cited results are stated without factual error or concept conflation.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by da, also methodology): This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence offered actually supports each claim made, and whether any fallacy undercuts the main argument. From my vantage, coherence failures often surface as domain mis-statements, but scoring belongs to the devil's advocate and methodology seats.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, owned by perspective): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions, and implications are legible to readers in neighboring fields and whether any interdisciplinary claims are substantiated. I do not score it; I flag only domain-internal accuracy that might bear on it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by eic): This dimension concerns organisation, clarity of exposition, quality of figures and tables, and conformity to venue formatting conventions. It is outside my remit.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by eic): This dimension asks whether the work suits the target venue and offers an original, significant contribution for its audience. Although my domain assessment informs what is genuinely new relative to prior work, the score belongs to the editor-in-chief seat.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Faithful and correctly attributed representation of prior work on representational alignment, similarity measures, and uncertainty decomposition; precise use of terms such as epistemic, aleatoric, alignment, and agreement; claims calibrated to what the cited and reported evidence supports; coverage of key recent work and competing viewpoints.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing claim misstates a cited prior result, conflates distinct uncertainty or similarity concepts in a way that changes a conclusion, or omits directly competing prior work that already establishes or refutes the claimed finding, but the defect is correctable by revision.
what_triggers_warn: Imprecise or inconsistent terminology, secondhand or incomplete attribution, missing recent relevant references, or mild overstatement of how prior work relates to the contribution, none of which alters a core conclusion.
what_triggers_fatal: The core thesis depends on a domain-level factual error or a fundamental misreading of the uncertainty or representation-similarity literature such that the central claim cannot stand even after revision.
