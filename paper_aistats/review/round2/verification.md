## New problems introduced (or newly exposed) by the revision

1. **The scale-blindness argument undercuts the recommended alternative.** Abstract: "CKA is also blind to feature scale, which acts on EU as a change of prior." Rec. (i): "prefer whitened (CCA-type) indices". CCA-type indices and Sρ→0 are invariant to all invertible linear maps (§4.3), so they are equally blind to scale. Only Sρ at the head's ρ on unnormalised features is not. The paper never states this.
2. **"All indices" overclaim.** §4.4: "All indices predict AU agreement about as well as EU agreement (Sρ: 0.92)". Table 5 contradicts this for linear predictivity: AU 0.53 against partial EU 0.83.
3. **The closed-form numbers in the prose do not match Table 5.** §4.4: "Sρ ranks the closed-form Pearson agreement at 0.93 (CKA: 0.74)". Table 5's "closed form" column gives Sρ 0.92 and CKA 0.76. The prose presumably uses a Pearson-agreement variant that appears in no table.
4. **Label confusion.** The revision introduces "E2-b" (AU falsifier) alongside "E2b"/"E2b-scale"/"E2b-tail" (transformations), as described under S2.
5. **"Robust" overclaim from exploratory evidence.** §5: "Two further findings are robust: ... even identical features leave the residual EU ranking dependent on the training data beyond estimator noise". This rests on post-review, exploratory redraws (3 draws, no intervals, reliability-vs-M curve from one encoder). §5 Limitations itself calls these analyses exploratory.
6. **Small inconsistencies.** "0.83–0.83" (§4.4). Fig. 3a / §4.2 give the DINOv2 re-test partial at M = 50 as 0.60, but Table 6 gives 0.61. Table 1 marks E2-a "supported" while its own footnote says the falsifier was uninformative.
7. **The abstract's CI scope is broader than the reported CI.** The abstract attributes the "(0.00 to 0.50)" difference interval to "CCA-type indices and linear predictivity", but it is reported only for Sρ − CKA.

**Three most important residual issues**

1. **R7 / §5 "robust":** the identical-features disagreement claim rests on an exploratory reliability correction. Reliability vs M is shown for one encoder only, and the 3 redraws have no intervals. Yet §5 calls the finding "robust" and the abstract states it without hedging.
2. **Scale-invariance logic (R8 residual):** CKA's scale-blindness is presented as a reason it fails, but the recommended CCA-type indices share that blindness. Rec. (i) needs to separate what whitened indices fix (tail weighting) from what only Sρ at the head's ρ fixes (prior–scale mismatch). The sweep that would support the latter (R3) is inconclusive.
3. **The empirical EU-specificity is still unestablished.** The paper now concedes this (R1). But the abstract and Table 5 headline still present confidence-controlled "EU agreement" ordering, while AU agreement is predicted equally well. The "All indices predict AU ... as well" sentence also overstates the case (linear predictivity AU 0.53). For softmax probes, the paper's empirical contribution reduces to head agreement in general.
